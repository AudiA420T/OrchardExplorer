📋 Data Notes & Known Limitations

🌍 World View

  • Source: FAO FAOSTAT 2022 (most recent available) — 192 countries.
  • ~8 countries (incl. Nigeria) only report Citrus (Total) to FAO — individual orange/grapefruit/etc. breakdown unavailable for those countries.
  • Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macao report separately from mainland China — this matches FAO's own structure.
  • FAO does not track sod/turfgrass — sod is US-only.

🇺🇸 US by State

  • Primary source: USDA NASS 2024 Annual Summaries (Noncitrus Fruits & Nuts; Citrus Fruits).
  • Secondary source: USDA Census of Agriculture 2022 — used for Sod, Coffee, and Hawaii citrus.
  • Wine/Table/Raisin grapes only cover 4 states (CA, WA, OR, NY) — states like VA, MI, TX, CO, and PA have commercial production not included in the NASS Annual Summary.
  • Hawaii citrus (9,649 acres) is bearing + non-bearing combined; all other states show bearing-only — slightly overstates HI relative to others.
  • Most tree/nut crops show only the major commercial producing states — this reflects true geographic concentration, not missing data.
  • Sod acreage (~373K acres, 44 states) is close to the national Census total of 376K; ~3,300 acres are withheld due to Census privacy rules.

🍊 California by County

  • Source: USDA Census of Agriculture 2022, Table 31.
  • Del Norte and Trinity counties show zero — they genuinely have no reportable orchard acreage per Census.
  • Grape acreage is reported as a single total — no wine/table/raisin breakdown available at the county level.
  • Sod and Coffee are not included in Census Table 31 for California counties.
